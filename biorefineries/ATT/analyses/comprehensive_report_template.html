ATT S1–S3 technical report

Alkaline thermal treatment of Sargassum presscake

Overall Results

The ATT model evaluates alkaline thermal treatment of wet Sargassum presscake for hydrogen production, sodium recovery and calcium carbonate (CaCO₃) production. S1–S3 share the core process and differ in external energy supply and heat integration.

S1 · Land-Based Baseline

Land-based ATT with natural gas, purchased electricity and existing internal heat recovery. External waste heat integration is absent.

S2 · Marine Waste Heat Recovery

ATT uses recovered ship-engine heat to replace part of purchased heating. The configuration includes marine heat recovery equipment and auxiliary power.

S3 · Industrial Waste Heat Recovery

Land-based ATT uses heat from a hypothetical calcium carbide facility to replace part of purchased steam. The configuration includes heat integration equipment and auxiliary power.

REE On adds rare earth element recovery and an REO-containing coproduct. REE Off uses the core ATT process without that route.

Understanding the Metrics

Hydrogen minimum selling price (MSP)

Discounted cash flow (DCF) solves for the H₂ price that meets the configured financial target. It accounts for operating expenses, investment, coproduct revenue, taxes, depreciation, financing and startup. MSP is a modeled threshold in USD/kg H₂. Observed market prices are outside this calculation.

Annualized net treatment cost

Operating cost + annualized capital cost − coproduct revenue. This screening convention excludes H₂ sales and uses simple annual capital recovery. Tables identify the denominator as t wet presscake or kg H₂.

Process LCA gross GWP100

Accounted fuel, electricity, chemicals and other process and upstream greenhouse gas burdens before biogenic carbon storage and avoided-production credits. Applicable WHR adjustments are already reflected in each scenario’s process inventory.

Underlying process LCA GWP100

Process LCA gross GWP100 plus the signed biogenic storage credit. This intermediate result retains the applicable WHR adjustment and precedes GCC and REO displacement.

Net GWP100

Underlying process LCA GWP100 − avoided conventional GCC production − potential avoided conventional mixed REO production. The REO credit applies when recovery is enabled. This is the principal reported GWP100.

Net GWP100 including potential beach avoidance

Net GWP100 − potential avoided methane emissions from a hypothetical beach-decomposition counterfactual. This separate estimate depends on aerobic and anaerobic allocation, biodegradability and methane realization. Storage and coproduct credits each enter once.

External-energy return on investment (EROI)

Contained H₂ lower heating value (LHV) output ÷ burdened external operational energy input, in MJ/MJ. Recovered host heat carries zero upstream energy burden under the adopted cut-off. Feedstock chemical energy, chemical embodied energy and construction energy are excluded.

The comparison and dashboard use kg CO₂e/kg H₂. Scenario tables show all four GWP100 layers and label the selected functional unit.

Common boundaries and assumptions

Feed and hydrogen basis

All scenarios share the modeled wet presscake composition and core ATT reaction. The modeled inlet contains water by mass. Dry feed includes ash. Results use contained H₂ mass. The active configurations have no pressure swing adsorption (PSA) step.

The separate fresh-Sargassum dataset uses moisture. Supporting energy material retains a legacy 87.5 wt% equivalent-mass reference.

Sodium recovery and CaCO₃ sales

The model applies causticization conversion and whole-liquor recycle. CaCO₃ revenue applies to contained saleable material. The remaining cake mass follows the configured disposal convention.

Storage and coproduct displacement

The process LCA assumes long-term retention of eligible biogenic carbon in carbonate and char. The report assumes displacement of conventional ground calcium carbonate (GCC) by recovered CaCO₃. For REE On, it applies kg CO₂e/kg contained REO at a substitution fraction. Commercial equivalence and upgrading requirements remain unverified. Wan et al. (2022).

Beach-decomposition boundary

The separate counterfactual models natural decomposition of the feedstock. Methane realization and biodegradability are screening assumptions. The potential credit is a modeled difference and lacks a measured beach-emissions baseline.

Unresolved Organic Matter and Carbon Fate
ResidueCH (CH₀.₅) and ResidueCHO (CH₀.₅O₂) are balancing components created in U2. RS1 sends the remaining material to att_residue_untracked; its simulator phase label is not evidence of a real disposal pathway. The baseline assigns no disposal cost, treatment emissions or storage credit to it. Its fate and disposition are pending further product-yield information from UCLA. The optional $0.04/kg disposal sensitivity is separate from the baseline.

The unresolved mass affects potential treatment costs, wastewater COD, methane emissions and the assumed thermochemical properties that determine reactor heat. Required measurements include matched DAF gas yields, dissolved organic carbon and COD, solid and liquid composition, char yield, degradation and methane potential.

Carbon boundary ledger and pseudo-component trace
Sodium and calcium cycles

The experimentally confirmed dosing basis is 1 g NaOH per g total dry Sargassum, not DAF. Gross reactor NaOH includes recycled alkali and differs from fresh makeup. The baseline keeps 100% assumed NaOH-to-carbonate conversion, UCLA's 88% causticization conversion and 95% whole-liquor recycle, interpreted from an incompletely specified experimental recovery. Waste carbide lime is 40% water, 92% Ca(OH)2 in dry solids and 55.2% Ca(OH)2 in wet paste. The simulated cake purity is retained; clean-laboratory 97% CaCO3 is a comparison only. F2 wash liquor returns internally to D1.

Independent DCF check and financial treatment

The DCF uses 10% IRR over 20 operating years, MACRS7 depreciation, 21% tax, 40%/60% construction spending, 5% working capital with terminal recovery, 60% debt at 8% over 10 years, and 3 startup months (FOC 100%, VOC/sales 50%). Depreciation affects taxable earnings; a separate screening capital charge is never added to the DCF. Negative-price wastes and net cake prices enter BioSTEAM as sales adjustments; the report separates disposal and revenue for readability. The discounted ledger below is computed from actual cash flows and startup profiles, to solver tolerance.

Annual treatment economics · selected case

No disposal fee is charged to untracked residue in the baseline. REO content is optimistically sold at $35/kg REO without additional upgrading costs. CaCO3 revenue is based on component mass, with non-saleable cake mass charged for disposal. Waste carbide lime is free; no verified Louisville lime supply is assumed.

Capital charge is FCI × capital recovery factor at the configured IRR and lifetime. Working capital, tax, debt and startup are excluded from this simple screening cost. H₂ MSP remains a separate DCF result.
