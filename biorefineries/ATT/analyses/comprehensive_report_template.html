One process. Three energy settings.

Unresolved Organic Matter and Carbon Fate

ResidueCH (CH₀.₅) and ResidueCHO (CH₀.₅O₂) are balancing components created in U2. RS1 sends the remaining material to att_residue_untracked; its simulator phase label is not evidence of a real disposal pathway. The baseline assigns no disposal cost, treatment emissions or storage credit to it. Its fate and disposition are pending further product-yield information from UCLA. The optional $0.04/kg disposal sensitivity is separate from the baseline.

The unresolved mass affects potential treatment costs, wastewater COD, methane emissions and the assumed thermochemical properties that determine reactor heat. Required measurements include matched DAF gas yields, dissolved organic carbon and COD, solid and liquid composition, char yield, degradation and methane potential.

Sodium and calcium cycles

The experimentally confirmed dosing basis is 1 g NaOH per g total dry Sargassum, not DAF. Gross reactor NaOH includes recycled alkali and differs from fresh makeup. The baseline keeps 100% assumed NaOH-to-carbonate conversion, UCLA's 88% causticization conversion and 95% whole-liquor recycle, interpreted from an incompletely specified experimental recovery. Waste carbide lime is 40% water, 92% Ca(OH)2 in dry solids and 55.2% Ca(OH)2 in wet paste. The simulated cake purity is retained; clean-laboratory 97% CaCO3 is a comparison only. F2 wash liquor returns internally to D1.

Independent DCF check and financial treatment

The DCF uses 10% IRR over 20 operating years, MACRS7 depreciation, 21% tax, 40%/60% construction spending, 5% working capital with terminal recovery, 60% debt at 8% over 10 years, and 3 startup months (FOC 100%, VOC/sales 50%). Depreciation affects taxable earnings; a separate screening capital charge is never added to the DCF. Negative-price wastes and net cake prices enter BioSTEAM as sales adjustments; the report separates disposal and revenue for readability. The discounted ledger below is computed from actual cash flows and startup profiles, to solver tolerance.

Annual treatment economics · selected case

No disposal fee is charged to untracked residue in the baseline. REO content is optimistically sold at $35/kg REO without additional upgrading costs. CaCO3 revenue is based on component mass, with non-saleable cake mass charged for disposal. Waste carbide lime is free; no verified Louisville lime supply is assumed.

Capital charge is FCI × capital recovery factor at the configured IRR and lifetime. Working capital, tax, debt and startup are excluded from this simple screening cost. H₂ MSP remains a separate DCF result.

Life-cycle assessment · GWP100

Baseline net GWP assumes permanent storage of eligible feed-derived carbon in carbonate cakes and char. Permanence and product end use are not established. No CaCO₃ substitution or avoided beach decomposition credit is added to baseline GWP. HCl and oxalic acid manufacture use provisional source-based factors; untracked organics receive no treatment emissions or storage credit. The ledger distinguishes process fuel combustion, upstream supply, waste management, storage and WHR adjustments.
