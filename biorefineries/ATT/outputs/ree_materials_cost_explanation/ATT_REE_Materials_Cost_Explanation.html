Why Does REE Recovery Increase Materials Cost?

REE recovery increases annual materials cost primarily through additional chemical consumption, especially HCl, rather than waste treatment expenses.

Why Is HCl Consumption Higher Than the Experimental Dosage?

Approximately 63% of modeled HCl demand is associated with neutralizing residual sodium carbonate, rather than the original experimental leaching dosage.

A Experiment-based leaching dose

  • UCLA procedure: 5 mL of 0.1 M HCl/g insoluble residue = 0.5 mol HCl/kg.
  • Applied to the model's insoluble residue/char basis: 758.71 kg/h, excluding dissolved salts and water.
  • Requires 44.62 kg/h of 31 wt% HCl solution.

The experimental dose is not the stoichiometric acid consumption of REE ions.

B Additional carbonate neutralization

  • Residual Na2CO3 entering LC1: 34.66 kg/h.
  • The model adds acid to neutralize carbonate before or during leaching: 0.654 kmol HCl/h.
  • Requires 76.92 kg/h of 31 wt% HCl solution.

Na2CO3 + 2 HCl → 2 NaCl + CO2 + H2O
